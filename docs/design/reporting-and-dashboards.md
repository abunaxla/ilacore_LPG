# Reporting and dashboards

## Dashboards by role
Each role has its own dashboard: owner (distributor), finance, depot/filling, driver, field supervisor,
investigations, wholesaler and retailer. Dashboards are built from the user's permissions and location scope.
Nobody sees a figure they aren't allowed to see.

The **owner view** shows every owned cylinder wherever it is, across four separate states:
fill state, custody/location, safety eligibility and security status (security only for permitted users).

## Rules for reliable, easy-to-follow reports
- **Every figure drills down to its source records.** For example, "KES 412,000 owed by Wholesaler A" → invoices → deliveries →
  cylinder IDs scanned. A report never holds a number of its own.
- Reports are computed from the ledger and event history, not from separately maintained totals.
- Every report shows its date range, filters, run time and who ran it.
- Exports to PDF, Excel and CSV carry the same figures as the screen.
- Plain language and consistent terms. Status is never shown by colour alone.
